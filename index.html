BNP-ISBA. The Bayesian Nonparametrics Section of the International Society for Bayesian Analysis.

BNP-ISBA is a section of the International Society for Bayesian Analysis (ISBA) aiming to promote the development and application of Bayesian nonparametric methods and inference techniques.

To this aim, we organise the biennial International Conference on Bayesian Nonparametrics and, in alternate years, the biennial Bayesian Nonparametric Networking Workshop. We also hold occasional workshops and sessions at other major conferences, along with a regular webinar series.

Join BNP-ISBA

Are you interested in Bayesian nonparametrics? Would you like to take part in activities that promote your research and keep you up to date with the latest developments in the field? Join BNP-ISBA!

We welcome researchers at any level. To become a member, subscribe to ISBA here. In the membership form you can add a one-year membership to BNP-ISBA for only $5 more, or a lifetime membership for $75.

To stay up to date, sign up to our BNP-ISBA Google group. Please include your name and affiliation when requesting to join.

Events

We organise the biennial International Conference on Bayesian Nonparametrics, showcasing the latest research and fostering collaborations. With several successful editions behind us, we are one of the largest sections of ISBA.

We also run the BNP networking workshops. This series of smaller, flexible workshops is designed to (a) create an informal BNP event with slots dedicated to early career researchers, (b) establish and strengthen local networks and broaden appeal, and (c) foster brainstorming and innovation.

Check out past and upcoming events organised or endorsed by BNP-ISBA.

Webinars

Attend our webinar series to find out about the latest research in Bayesian nonparametrics and to learn how the fundamental ideas of the field were developed.

Webinars run roughly every two months and recordings of past talks are available.
